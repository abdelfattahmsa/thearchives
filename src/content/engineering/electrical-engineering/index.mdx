# Electrical Engineering

Electrical engineering deals with the study, design, and application of electrical systems, electronic components, and electromagnetic fields.

## Disciplines

- **Electronic Engineering** — circuits, semiconductors, and electronic devices
- **Computer Engineering** — hardware/software integration and digital systems
- **Power Engineering** — generation, transmission, and distribution of electrical power
- **Optical Engineering** — light-based systems, lasers, and photonics
