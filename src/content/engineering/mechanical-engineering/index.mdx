# Mechanical Engineering

Mechanical engineering applies physics and materials science to design, analyse, manufacture, and maintain mechanical systems.

## Disciplines

- **Acoustical Engineering** — sound and vibration control in systems and environments
- **Manufacturing Engineering** — processes and systems for producing goods
- **Optomechanical Engineering** — optical instruments and precision mechanisms
- **Thermal Engineering** — heat transfer, thermodynamics, and energy systems
- **Sports Engineering** — performance analysis and equipment design for sport
- **Vehicle Engineering** — design and development of land, sea, and air vehicles
- **Power Plant Engineering** — design and operation of energy generation facilities
