# Construction Management

Construction management covers the planning, coordination, and control of a project from inception to completion, on behalf of the client.

## Project Phases

1. **Inception & Feasibility** — project brief, site surveys, feasibility study
2. **Design** — concept → schematic → detailed design → tender documentation
3. **Procurement** — tendering, contractor selection, contract award
4. **Construction** — site mobilization, progress monitoring, quality control
5. **Handover & Closeout** — snagging, O&M manuals, defects liability period

## Contracts

### Common Forms
- **FIDIC** — international standard contracts (Red, Yellow, Silver books)
- **NEC4** — collaborative contract widely used in UK infrastructure
- **JCT** — Joint Contracts Tribunal, standard for UK building works

### Key Concepts
- Contract sum vs. final account
- Variations and change orders
- Extension of time (EOT) claims
- Liquidated damages (LD)

## Quality Management
- ISO 9001 — Quality Management Systems
- Inspection and Test Plans (ITP)
- Non-conformance reports (NCR)
