# Civil Engineering

Civil engineering encompasses the design, construction, and maintenance of the built and natural environment — from infrastructure to buildings.

## Disciplines

- **Project Controls** — scheduling, cost management, and performance monitoring
- **BIM** — Building Information Modelling for integrated project delivery
- **CAD** — Computer-Aided Design tools and workflows
- **Construction Management** — planning, coordination, and execution of construction projects
- **Environmental Engineering** — protecting and improving the natural environment
- **Geotechnical Engineering** — soil, rock, and foundation systems
- **Structural Engineering** — load-bearing systems and structural integrity
- **Transport Engineering** — roads, railways, and transport networks
- **Utility Engineering** — water, gas, and electrical utility systems
- **Water Resources Engineering** — hydrology, drainage, and water infrastructure
- **Architectural Engineering** — technical systems within buildings
