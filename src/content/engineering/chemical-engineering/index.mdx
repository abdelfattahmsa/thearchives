# Chemical Engineering

Chemical engineering applies principles of chemistry, physics, and biology to design processes that convert raw materials or chemicals into useful forms.

## Disciplines

- **Biomolecular Engineering** — engineering at the molecular scale using biological systems
- **Materials Engineering** — properties, processing, and performance of materials
- **Molecular Engineering** — design and synthesis of molecules for specific functions
- **Process Engineering** — industrial processes for chemical production
- **Corrosion Engineering** — prevention and control of material degradation
