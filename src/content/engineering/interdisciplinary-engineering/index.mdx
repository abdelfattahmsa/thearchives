# Interdisciplinary Engineering

Interdisciplinary engineering fields draw from multiple traditional disciplines to solve complex problems that span boundaries.

## Disciplines

- **Agricultural Engineering** — technology and systems for farming and food production
- **Energy Engineering** — sustainable and efficient energy systems
- **Applied Engineering** — practical application of engineering principles
- **Biomedical Engineering** — medical devices, prosthetics, and healthcare technology
- **Biological Engineering** — engineering using biological organisms and systems
- **Building Services Engineering** — MEP systems within buildings (HVAC, plumbing, electrical)
- **Information Engineering** — information systems design and management
- **Industrial Engineering** — optimisation of complex processes and organisations
- **Mechatronics Engineering** — integration of mechanical, electrical, and software systems
- **Engineering Management** — leadership and management within engineering contexts
- **Military Engineering** — engineering in defence and military operations
- **Mining Engineering** — extraction and processing of mineral resources
- **Nanoengineering** — engineering at the nanoscale
- **Quantum Engineering** — quantum mechanics applied to computing and sensing
- **Nuclear Engineering** — nuclear reactions for power, medicine, and research
- **Petroleum Engineering** — exploration and extraction of oil and gas
- **Project Engineering** — technical management within engineering projects
- **Railway Engineering** — rail infrastructure, vehicles, and operations
- **Software Engineering** — systematic development of software systems
- **Supply Chain Engineering** — design and optimisation of supply chains
- **Systems Engineering** — holistic approach to complex system design
- **Textile Engineering** — fibres, fabrics, and textile manufacturing processes
