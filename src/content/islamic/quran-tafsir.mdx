# Quran & Tafsir

## The Quran

The Quran (القرآن) is the verbatim word of Allah revealed to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ over 23 years. It consists of:
- **114 Surahs** (chapters)
- **6,236 ayat** (verses)
- Revealed in **Makki** (before Hijra) and **Madani** (after Hijra) periods

## Quranic Sciences ('Ulum al-Quran)

| Science | Description |
|---|---|
| Asbab al-Nuzul | Occasions/reasons of revelation |
| Makki & Madani | Classification of surahs by place of revelation |
| Nasikh wal-Mansukh | Abrogating and abrogated verses |
| I'jaz al-Quran | The miraculous nature of the Quran |
| Tajweed | Rules of Quranic recitation |

## Tafsir

Tafsir (تفسير) is the scholarly interpretation and explanation of the Quran.

### Major Tafsir Works

| Scholar | Work | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Ibn Kathir (d. 774 AH) | *Tafsir Ibn Kathir* | Classical, widely used, hadith-based |
| al-Tabari (d. 310 AH) | *Jami' al-Bayan* | Most comprehensive classical tafsir |
| al-Qurtubi (d. 671 AH) | *al-Jami' li-Ahkam al-Quran* | Strong on fiqh (legal rulings) |
| Ibn Ashur (d. 1973) | *al-Tahrir wal-Tanwir* | Contemporary, linguistic depth |
| Sayyid Qutb (d. 1966) | *Fi Zilal al-Quran* | Contemporary, thematic approach |

### Methodology of Tafsir

1. **Tafsir bil-Ma'thur** — interpretation by transmitted narrations (Quran by Quran, then Sunnah, then Companions)
2. **Tafsir bil-Ra'y** — interpretation by reasoned opinion (must be grounded in Arabic language and Islamic sciences)
