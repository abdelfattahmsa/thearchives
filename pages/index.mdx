## What Is The Archives?

The Archives is the knowledge layer of [Peridot Holdings](https://peridot.holdings) — a structured wiki built to:

- **Document** knowledge across engineering, technology, design, and Islamic studies
- **Reference** sources clearly so you can always go deeper
- **Share** freely — everything here is open to read and use

This is not a blog. It is a living reference — built to last, updated as knowledge grows.
